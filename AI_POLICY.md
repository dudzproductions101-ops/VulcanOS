## AI Policy

### We Like AI, But We Prefer Human Code

At VulcanOS, we appreciate AI as a useful tool for development. AI can be used for small tasks such as writing code snippets, helping with debugging, researching ideas, or improving documentation.
However, AI-generated work must always be reviewed and edited by a real person before being added to the project. Contributors should understand the code they submit and make sure it meets the quality, security, and style standards of VulcanOS.
AI is here to help developers, not replace them. Human decision-making, testing, and review are required to keep the project reliable and maintainable.
